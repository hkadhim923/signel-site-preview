What warranty do Signel Services signs carry?

All our products are made right here in our plant. Sheeting: Engineer grade: High-intensity grade: Diamond Grade: Vinyl-type sheeting: All our sheeting is 3M sheeting, the most reflective there is

Can I get more runtime between battery charges, or before the good weather returns, on equipment such as radar signs, variable message signs (VMS) or work zone lights?

Yes! Almost all our electronic equipment models can have their runtime extended or improved by adding more batteries, higher-capacity batteries or solar panels more powerful than the original. Just contact the sales department

My radar sign no longer shows messages, only speeds; OR it shows only speeds above the programmed limit, with neither messages nor low speeds. Is something wrong?

Almost certainly not! All Signel solar systems are designed to protect and so preserve the batteries, sparing the customer needless expense if they run down too far. The RAD50 and RAD54 radar signs automatically stop showing messages and ''acceptable'' speeds below certain battery voltage levels set by the customer through the programming tablet; (''Power saving'' mode in the software.) Give the sun time (1 to 2 days of fine weather) to do its work before concluding the system has an electronic fault

The brightness on my radar sign / VMS / light display seems lower than it used to be. Is that possible?

Possibly! Check that the ambient light sensor (LDR) is not blocked by debris, dirt or permanent shade over it (from a tree, for instance); the LDR looks like a small camera lens and sits on the front of the radar signs and work zone lights, and on top of the VMS

The speed and/or message display on my radar sign shows figures or messages constantly and at random. Is it faulty?

No! There are two possibilities here; 1- in the display settings on your tablet, ''Demonstration'' mode may be ticked; or 2- the Doppler radar sensitivity has been set too high (above 2); other than in exceptional cases, if the sensitivity goes above 3 (of 1 to 5), an approaching vehicle's speed will appear on the display before the driver can see or read it
