Vinyl Letterer

HomeOpen positionsVinyl Letterer

Posted August 5, 2026

  • Montréal, QC
  • Permanent position
  • 5 August 2026

Job description:

Signel Services is a Quebec company that has specialised in road signage for more than 30 years. We build signage products (signs, delineators, trailers, variable message signs, portable traffic signals for work zones and so on) and we offer services such as rental, arrow board installation and repair.

We are looking for a Vinyl Letterer to produce our road signs and create a variety of products!

To apply you can do so through Indeed at the following link: https://ca.indeed.com/job/lettreurlettreuse-de-vinyle-4644eefafad1ead4 or apply directly on this page by clicking "Apply now".

  • Follow the drawing, the various instructions and the production processes for the products; 
  • Transfer the pictogram onto a plastic, vinyl or aluminum surface; 
  • Apply, weed and cut the vinyl; 
  • Work as a team with the graphic design department to produce the signs; 
  • Help build the road signs; 
  • Handle the finished products; 
  • Any other related duties may be assigned to meet the needs of the company. 

Skills and qualities we are looking for:

  • Have a basic knowledge of lettering or vinyl cutting (a strong asset); 
  • Posséder une grande minutie et beaucoup de dextérité manuelle ; 
  • Be able to spot small imperfections and have an eye for detail; 
  • Be able to lift weights of 2 lbs to 20 lbs to move the various materials and finished products; 
  • Must be able to handle basic tools: knife, square and other small tools, etc.; 
  • Must work at a good pace; 
  • Must be resourceful. 

Benefits:

  • Permanent position, full time, 40h a week; 
  • Day shift Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:30pm; 
  • Salary to be discussed based on experience and skills; 
  • Annual performance review leading to a salary increase each year; 
  • Monthly bonus available if the company's targets are met; 
  • Group insurance after 3 months; 
  • Vacation bank available from your first day; 
  • Employee assistance programme available; 
  • Great understanding and flexibility for family and personal commitments; 
  • The atmosphere of a family business, pleasant and lively; 
  • An employee committee organising company activities throughout the year; 
  • On-site training and ongoing training provided; 
  • Casual dress;
  • And plenty more!!! 

Experience:

  • None

Languages:

  • Command of spoken and written French.
