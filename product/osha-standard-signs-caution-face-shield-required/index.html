OSHA standard signs: Caution – Face shield required

SKU: DSO[M][F]2-017x · Product ID: 13976

OSHA STANDARD (Occupational Safety and Health Administration): Signs used to draw attention to any hazard that could cause accidental injury or damage to property. These signs deliver a clear message that is understood at a glance

Choice of materials:

  • Styrene (thickness 0.060'' (1.52 mm)): Semi-flexible, economical – The choice for indoors and for sheltered outdoor locations.
  • White pre-painted aluminum (thickness 0.064'' (1.63 mm)): Get the strength of metal without the trouble of rust. High resistance to abrasion and chemicals for indoor and outdoor use
  • Self-adhesive vinyl (thickness 0.060'' 0.003" (0.08 mm)) Goes on any curved surface such as pipes, posts and secondary containers. The choice for indoors and for sheltered outdoor locations.
