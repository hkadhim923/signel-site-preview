The speed reduction delineator is a simple, effective and inexpensive way to bring vehicle speeds down by 5 to 20 km/h.
It narrows the street and changes how drivers behave. A new sense of the space leads drivers to slow down.

Unlike a speed hump, this delineator does not slow emergency vehicles, and it encourages drivers into a low, steady speed. That style of driving keeps noise, air pollution and fuel use down.
If a vehicle strikes it, the delineator bends under the impact and then returns to its original position. It is built to survive any impact, even when hit at more than 80 km/h. Its rubber base lets it take the weight of heavy vehicles, even when a wheel runs straight over it.
In short, here are the 5 advantages of our speed reduction delineator over speed humps:
– It keeps noise down
– It does not slow emergency vehicles
– It creates no vibration
– It reduces air pollution and fuel use
– It creates no snow-clearing problem, because the delineators are taken away over the winter.
