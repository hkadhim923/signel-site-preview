Signel Services at the heart of the 2025 Montreal Marathon

News · September 23, 2025

This weekend our teams had the privilege of taking part in the 2025 Montreal Marathon, an event that brought together more than 5,500 runners to take on this sporting challenge in the heart of the city.

We are proud to have helped the race run smoothly, putting our road-safety expertise to work. All weekend our teams worked tirelessly to make sure the participants could run in an environment that was safe, free-flowing and well managed.

Thanks to everyone's commitment and professionalism, we were able to support the organisers in their mission: to give the runners a memorable experience while protecting spectators and road users alike.

 Congratulations to everyone who crossed the finish line, and thank you to the organisers for trusting us with an event of this scale.

We are always proud to put our know-how to work for the safety and success of major events like this one, while playing our part in the life of our community.

thank you to courons montréal for their trust over many years!
