PRIVACY AND PERSONAL INFORMATION
PROTECTION POLICY OF SIGNEL SERVICES INC.
(the "Policy")

1. INTRODUCTION

Respect for your privacy and the protection of your personal information matter a great deal to SIGNEL SERVICES INC., its subsidiaries and management companies (together "Signel ", "our", "we" or "us"). We have therefore put this Policy in place to govern the collection, use, disclosure, retention and destruction of personal information, whether or not it was collected by technological means. This Policy also describes how we protect personal information and the process we have set up for handling complaints.

2. APPLICATION AND DEFINITIONS

This Policy applies to Signel, which includes its officers, employees and consultants as well as anyone else who provides services for Signel’s benefit.

It covers the personal information of customers (prospective, current or past), of visitors to Signel’s website at www.signel.ca (the "Site") and of any other individual who provides personal information to Signel.

For the purposes of this Policy, and as defined in the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (the "Act"), personal information is information that concerns an individual and allows that person to be identified, directly or indirectly. It could be, for example, a person’s first and last name, postal address, email address, telephone number, gender, banking details, information about their health, their ethnic origin or their language, information about their use of our Site including technical information about visits or anything else collected through cookies, and any other personal information you consider useful to give us (the "Personal Information").

A person’s professional or business contact details are not considered Personal Information, nor is personal information that is public in nature.

BY PROVIDING PERSONAL INFORMATION TO SIGNEL OR TO ITS SUPPLIERS, THROUGH OUR SITE, BY TELEPHONE, BY EMAIL, IN PERSON OR BY ANY OTHER MEANS, YOU CONSENT TO YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION BEING COLLECTED, USED, DISCLOSED, RETAINED AND DESTROYED AS SET OUT IN THIS POLICY.

3. CONSENT, COLLECTION, USE AND DISCLOSURE

3.1. Consent

As a general rule, Signel will obtain Personal Information directly from you, with your consent, subject to the exceptions set out in the Act. Personal Information will be used only for the purposes for which it was collected unless we obtain your consent.

If you consent, or where the law permits it, Signel may also collect Personal Information from third parties.

Signel will not collect, use or disclose Personal Information without your consent unless it is authorised or required to do so under the Act.

Note that you have the right to refuse to give us Personal Information. You also have the right, subject to reasonable notice and to any applicable legal restrictions, to withdraw your consent to the use of Personal Information already collected, by contacting our privacy officer using the details in section 15. Please note, however, that refusing to provide information or withdrawing your consent could prevent us from providing, or continuing to provide, our services or information about them.

IMPORTANT: If you give us Personal Information about other people, you represent that you have the authority to do so and, where applicable, that you have obtained the necessary consent, and you acknowledge that this information may be used in accordance with this Policy.

3.2. Collection and use

Without limiting the scope of what follows, Signel will collect and use Personal Information for the purposes identified at the time the information is collected, subject to compliance with the Act. Collection will be limited to the information that is necessary for those purposes. Below is a non-exhaustive list of the information Signel may collect. Apart from the Personal Information of certain customers and the information that may be collected when you use our Site, most of the information Signel collects and manages is professional in nature.

3.2.1. Site users, or interaction with us by email

When you browse our Site or interact with us by email, we may collect information about you, as described below:

  • When you make an enquiry, we may collect information about you such as your first and last name, telephone number, email address, your specific request and any other information or document you think it useful to send us.
  • When you create an online customer account, we may collect information about you such as your first and last name, address, telephone number and a password for accessing your account. Note that accounts are intended mainly for businesses, but it is still possible to create one as an individual.
  • Connection data, which may include your IP address, the pages you visit on the Site, the date and time of your visit to the Site, the address of the web page you visited before using the Site, the URL that directed you to our Site, your browser type and settings, information about your device (such as make, model and operating system), information about your browser configuration, language preferences, unique identifiers and cookies.
  • Usage data and analytics, which may include how often you connect, the features you use, how long and when you use them, and the different types of activity users undertake.
  • If you would like further information about our services, want to use our features, sign up for an event, a webinar or a contest, or download certain content, we may ask you for your contact details, such as your name, telephone number and email address, along with some information about your needs or preferences.
  • Customer support questions and any general comments you choose to provide.
  • Any other Personal Information provided.

This information is collected to help us learn more about your needs, preferences and interactions with our Site and the services offered, to produce statistics and market and performance studies that help us assess our Site and our services, to measure our performance and to improve the experiences and services we offer. It also serves to identify you for account access when you create one.

3.2.2. Customers (prospective, current or past)

When you ask for information about our products and services, request a quote and/or buy products, we may collect information about you, as described below:

  • Your first and last name, postal address, telephone number, email, banking details, language and anything else considered relevant.

This information is collected in order to:

  • Manage your file, communicate with you and maintain your customer account;
  • Process payments;
  • Send information about the various products and services on offer, or the information you have asked for, and to answer your requests and questions;
  • Communicate with you (by telephone, alert, email, post or otherwise) and present you with advertising material and further information about our services, products, surveys, contests, promotions and special offers, as well as providing our customer service;
  • Produce statistics and market and performance studies that help us assess our services, measure our performance and improve the experiences and services we offer.

3.2.3. Surveys, events and marketing information

If you choose to take part in our surveys, contests, events and information requests, we may collect information about you in connection with the survey, contest, information request or event, including your name, email address and telephone number, and general information about you that you choose to provide. We may also use your personal information for marketing purposes.

3.2.4. Telephone calls

If you contact us by telephone, we may record the call and ask you for your contact details, such as your name, address, telephone number and email address. This information is used to identify you so that we can serve you better.

3.2.5. Visits to our offices

If you visit our offices, you may be asked to sign in as a visitor and to give your name, email address and telephone number, along with the time and date you arrive and leave. This information is gathered for security reasons, so that we know who is coming and going on Signel’s premises.

3.3. Disclosure

Signel limits the disclosure of your Personal Information for purposes other than those mentioned above, but note the following points and exceptions:

3.3.1 Law enforcement

We may share your Personal Information in response to law enforcement requests, court orders or other legal proceedings, or if we believe such disclosure is necessary to investigate, prevent or respond to illegal activity, fraud or physical threats, or as required by any other applicable law or regulation, and to the extent necessary to protect Signel’s property, interests and rights.

3.3.2 Archiving service

We may share your Personal Information with a business whose activity consists of preserving documents for their general informational value.

3.3.3. Our employees and authorised service providers

Our employees may have access to your Personal Information in order to help us provide you with services. They access and use that Personal Information in accordance with our instructions, only where they need to know it, and under strict confidentiality and security obligations. Please be assured that we make sure our employees handle this information with all the discretion and care required, and comply with this Policy and with the legal and regulatory requirements in force.

When you provide Personal Information to Signel, you give us the right to share your Personal Information with authorised service providers we consider acceptable, who will use that information to help us answer your requests and perform services. Please note that we will share only the Personal Information necessary for the use of those third-party services, solely for the purpose of providing you with the services, and that we will make sure those authorised providers agree to keep that information confidential and secure. Service providers retain the information they need to perform the functions they were appointed for, and we do not allow those third parties to use or disclose your Personal Information for their own marketing or for any other purpose.

3.3.4. Commercial transaction

Signel could be involved (1) in the sale or lease of all or substantially all of its operations and/or the assets it holds, (2) in a change to its legal structure by merger or otherwise, (3) in a transaction to obtain a loan or any other form of financing, or (4) in any other similar transaction (individually and together the "Transaction"). As part of such a Transaction, we may transfer or disclose your Personal Information to the other organisation, current or prospective, but we will require that organisation to agree to protect the confidentiality of your Personal Information in a manner equivalent to this Policy.

3.3.5. Statistics and aggregated reports

We may use Personal Information in de-identified form, for example for statistics, analysis, calibration, management and quality improvement in relation to the Site and the services offered on it, or for the development or provision of other products and services by Signel or for the benefit of all Site users generally. Signel guarantees, however, that such aggregated and de-identified data can never make it possible to identify you in any way.

3.3.6. Express consent

Signel may also disclose your Personal Information if you ask it to or expressly consent.

5. TRANSFER OF PERSONAL INFORMATION OUTSIDE QUEBEC

We may in future engage third-party service providers to process, manage and host Personal Information on our behalf, and they may be located outside Quebec. Some Personal Information about you may therefore be transferred to another jurisdiction and be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

We do, however, make sure we protect the Personal Information in our care, including Personal Information entrusted to a provider whether it is located in Quebec or abroad. Our personal information protection policies and practices require such a service provider to sign a written undertaking to meet its obligation to preserve the confidentiality and security of the Personal Information entrusted to it. That includes putting robust, effective security measures in place, and also a prohibition on disclosing, transferring or using your Personal Information for anyone else, in accordance with the legislation applicable in the province of Quebec on the protection of personal information.

6. PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

We take appropriate steps to ensure the confidentiality of Personal Information and to protect that Personal Information against loss, and against unauthorised access, disclosure, copying, use or modification, taking into account in particular the sensitivity of the information and the purposes for which it is intended.

Signel uses commercially reasonable and appropriate physical, electronic and management procedures, and generally accepted industry standards, to safeguard and secure the Personal Information we collect.

The Personal Information we hold is therefore accessible only to people who are qualified to see it and who consult it only where it is necessary in the course of their duties.

To that end, we make sure among other things to restrict access to our offices and computer equipment, to train our staff, and to require our agents and suppliers of goods and services who have access to Personal Information to sign confidentiality agreements and to put in place security measures considered equivalent.

Note, however, that no mechanism offers total security; in particular, using the Internet to transmit, create or host Personal Information always carries some risk.

If you believe your Personal Information has been compromised, please contact our privacy officer using the details in section 15 of this Policy.

7. MINORS

Signel does not knowingly collect Personal Information from people under 18.

If a minor under 14 wishes to use our Site or our services, or to contact us in any other way, that minor’s Personal Information cannot be collected without the consent of the person having parental authority or of the tutor. If we learn that we have unintentionally collected Personal Information from people under 14 without having obtained the consent of the person having parental authority or of the tutor, we will see to it that the information is deleted from our records as quickly as possible. If you are the person having parental authority or the tutor of a minor under 14 and you know that your minor child has provided Personal Information, please contact our privacy officer using the details in section 15 of this Policy so that we can take the necessary action.

8. RETENTION PERIOD

Apart from non-identifying, de-identified information that we may keep in perpetuity and in any reasonable place, Signel will host and retain your Personal Information for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy and to make sure we comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

If you would like to know more about how long your Personal Information is kept, please contact our privacy officer using the details in section 15 of this Policy.

9. ACCESS TO, RECTIFICATION AND UPDATING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION, AND WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

You have the right to access your Personal Information and to obtain copies of it. You also have the right to update, rectify and correct any Personal Information that you believe is inaccurate or incomplete. You may also require that the dissemination of Personal Information about you cease, or that any hyperlink attached to your name giving access to that Personal Information by technological means be de-indexed, where its dissemination contravenes the Act or a court order. You may do the same, or require that the hyperlink giving access to the Personal Information be re-indexed, where certain conditions set out in the Act are met.

To do so, please submit a written request to that effect to the privacy officer at the address given in section 15, who will reply to you in writing within 30 days of receiving any such request.

If you would like copies of your Personal Information, we may have to charge you a small fee, which will be disclosed to you beforehand, purely to cover the cost of transcription, reproduction and/or transmission.

Subject to any legal and contractual restrictions, you may at any time withdraw your consent to the use or disclosure of the Personal Information collected about you.

14. COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCEDURE

14.1.Scope

This procedure applies to the personal information held by Signel and to anyone who handles that information.

14.2.Confidential handling of the complaint

In handling complaints, Signel undertakes to respect the confidentiality and to ensure the protection of the personal information it holds throughout its life cycle, from collection through to destruction.

Every complaint is handled confidentially. Only the information necessary to handle the complaint will be shared, and only with the members of Signel staff who need to know it in the course of their duties.

14.3.Receipt of the complaint

Any complaint about the handling of personal information must be made in writing and addressed to the privacy officer by email at estdenis@signel.ca.

Any employee or associate who receives a complaint must inform the privacy officer immediately.

14.4.Admissibility of a complaint

For a complaint to be admissible, it must meet the following criteria:

  • it is made by an individual;
  • it concerns dissatisfaction with a practice, an action or an omission by Signel regarding the management or protection of the personal information it holds about that person;
  • it contains the following:
    • the first and last name and contact details of the person concerned;
    • a sufficiently precise description of the problem; and
    • the corrective measure or measures sought, if any.

Complaints that are anonymous, abusive, frivolous or defamatory, that contain hateful language, that are plainly unfounded or that do not contain the information needed to handle them may be rejected. Reasons must, however, be given to the person concerned.

14.5.Investigation and analysis of the complaint

A separate file will be created for each complaint received, where applicable. The file must contain the complaint, the analysis and the documentation supporting its assessment, and the reply sent to the person concerned.

The privacy officer conducts a thorough investigation, gathering evidence, interviewing the parties concerned and collecting all relevant documents.

The privacy officer must be impartial and have the authority needed to resolve the complaint.

14.6.Resolution of the complaint

14.6.1. Inadmissible complaint

The privacy officer informs the person concerned in writing when their complaint is inadmissible. The letter sets out why the complaint is not admissible.

14.6.2. Determining whether the complaint is well founded

Where the complaint is admissible, the privacy officer proceeds to handle it. After analysing it and gathering all the relevant facts, they determine whether or not it is well founded.

A complaint is well founded where the privacy officer concludes that there has been an error or a failing in relation to the laws, regulations or policies governing the management and protection of personal information by Signel.

If the complaint is well founded, the privacy officer proposes corrective measures or steps that must be carried out as quickly as possible.

A complaint made under this procedure does not allow the person concerned to obtain financial compensation.

14.7.Time limit for handling complaints

Signel will have thirty (30) days to analyse each complaint.

Signel undertakes to make every reasonable effort to handle each complaint within the thirty (30) day limit. Should the complaint not be capable of being handled within that time, however, the person concerned will be informed of the reasons justifying the extension, of the progress made in handling their complaint and of the reasonable time needed before a final answer can be given.

14.8.Communication with the person concerned

The privacy officer communicates regularly with the person concerned to keep them informed of the progress of the investigation and of the resolution of the complaint.

All communications must be professional, empathetic and respectful.

14.9.Closing the complaint

Once the complaint has been resolved, all information and documents relating to it must be kept in a confidential file to which only the privacy officer has access.

The privacy officer must keep and update a Register of complaints concerning the protection of personal information.

You may also file a written complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information – Direction de la surveillance at the following address: 900-2045, rue Stanley, Montréal, Québec, H3A 2V4, or by email at cai.communications@cai.gouv.qc.ca. We do, however, invite anyone interested to approach the privacy officer first and to wait until Signel has finished handling the complaint.
